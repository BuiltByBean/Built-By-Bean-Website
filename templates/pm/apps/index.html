Every tile is one height, a web app's (Michael, the same day: "the height of one like wedding planner ... should be the default height"). So nothing on a tile may add a line: the name truncates, a phone app's store versions sit on the host line beside the host, and a site that is not answering turns the host red rather than adding a pill. It is in two groups: what makes money (client builds and his own products that earn), then personal projects (Michael, 2026-11-04). A group with nothing in it is drawn only while a tile is being dragged, as somewhere to drop it: an empty heading at rest would be a claim about the business, not a state of the board. The edge down the left of each tile drags it to any place on the board, in its own half or the other (Michael, 2026-11-05: "move it up a row into the middle to where everything shifts"). While it is carried a gap stands where it will land and the other tiles slide out of the way; letting go puts it in the gap, and one POST to apps.order saves that half's order (AppLink.position) and, if it crossed, its half. The page order is position, then id, so a reload draws what was dropped. A tap on the edge moves nothing, so a stray thumb cannot move a tile; for the keyboard, Enter sends it to the other half and the arrows move it one place. Dragging is pointer events on the grip, not HTML5 drag and drop, which a phone does not do; touch-action none on the grip keeps a finger's drag from being taken as a scroll. The tile is not one big anchor.
